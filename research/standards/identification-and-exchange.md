# Warehouse identification, labels and partner messages

For the first site, make a scan answer three questions. Which object did the operator scan? What quantity and unit does it represent? Which details still need confirmation? A supplier can send a pallet identifier and expected contents in advance. The warehouse still needs to record what arrived and what it accepted. These technical standards provide common meanings. Applicability in Venezuela depends on the product, the operation and its trading agreements. [GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, §§3–4](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/logistic-label/) · [GS1 receiving advice](https://www.gs1.org/sites/default/files/docs/eancom/s3/recadv.pdf)

## What a scan identifies

| Object | Meaning under GS1 | Suggested WMS treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trade item | A Global Trade Item Number (GTIN) identifies a trade item at a defined packaging level. An each and an orderable case can have separate GTINs. | Map each GTIN to its package and unit conversion. A case scan should add the correct number of base units. Keep the internal SKU and supplier aliases. [GS1 master data guidance](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/gdm-implementation/1.2.0/) |
| Lot or individual unit | Application identifier (AI) 10 carries a batch or lot. AI 21 carries a serial. Each qualifies the corresponding GTIN. AI 15 means best before and AI 17 means expiry. | Keep the product with the lot or serial. Preserve both date meanings. Make required capture fields depend on the item and partner. [GS1 AI registry](https://ref.gs1.org/ai/?lang=en) · [GS1 General Specifications](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/) |
| Logistic unit | The Serial Shipping Container Code (SSCC) identifies one assembled unit, such as a pallet. AI 00 precedes its 18 digits. | Preserve the supplier’s SSCC for the life of that unit. Associate it with the receipt, contents and movements. Use a distinct internal handling-unit ID for an unlabelled pallet. [GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, §3](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/logistic-label/) |
| Location or party | A Global Location Number (GLN) identifies a party or location. Its business role gives it meaning. | Store the warehouse, ship-to party and receiving dock separately where the process distinguishes them. Start with site-local bin codes and map external GLNs when exchanged. [GS1 GLN allocation rules, §4.5](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/) |

Store identifiers as strings to preserve leading zeroes. Capture the scanner’s original output and symbology where available. EAN/UPC retail scans can give a GTIN alone. A GS1-128 or GS1 DataMatrix scan can supply several AIs. Use the GS1 syntax dictionary to validate field lengths, separators, check digits and AI associations. Preserve ASCII group separators from the device. An unknown scan can go to a human for identification while the receipt remains in progress. [GS1 barcode syntax resources](https://ref.gs1.org/tools/gs1-barcode-syntax-resource/) · [GS1 guide to barcode syntaxes, §7](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/2d-in-retail/)

An offline trial of the official GS1 engine showed that omitting a scanner separator can turn a lot and an expiry into one syntactically valid lot. It also distinguishes retail GTINs, pallet identifiers and the merchant’s internal bin codes. [Eight scan fixtures and observed parser results](barcode-capture-trial.md)

## Printing an outbound pallet label

On a GS1 Logistic Label, the SSCC is the sole universally required data element. The company building the pallet normally assigns it using its GS1 Company Prefix. For a homogeneous pallet, adding AI 02 for the contained trade item requires AI 37 for its count and AI 00 for the pallet. The label rules exclude combining AI 01 and AI 02. For a mixed pallet assembled for transport, identify the pallet by SSCC and exchange its item-level contents in the despatch advice. [GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, §§3–4, 10](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/logistic-label/)

Before enabling SSCC issuance, establish who controls the prefix and number allocation across sites. Reprinting a damaged label for the same intact pallet should reproduce its SSCC. Keep the relationship between the source pallet and any newly assembled units when repacking. Before rollout, verify label content, quiet zones, print quality and placement with actual stock, printers and receiving equipment. Get the customer's label layout and acceptance criteria. [GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, §§6–9 and Appendix A](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/logistic-label/)

## Traceability and electronic exchange

**Track a lot across custody changes.** The GS1 Global Traceability Standard describes critical tracking events and the data needed to answer who handled which goods, where, when and why. At receipt and shipment, link the item, lot or serial, quantity and unit, location, immediate supplier or recipient, and document reference. Preserve packing and unpacking relationships over time. A recall drill should find the supplier receipt, quantity still held by location, and downstream deliveries even after a pallet was split. [GS1 Global Traceability Standard, §§3–4](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/global-traceability/2.0.0/)

**Exchange business documents when a partner can act on them.** UN/EDIFACT DESADV is a despatch advice, often called an advance ship notice (ASN). RECADV reports physical receipt and may report discrepancies. A partner using GS1 EANCOM can specify its message version, packaging hierarchy and which references and discrepancy codes it accepts. Agree how to handle revisions, duplicates and acknowledgements. Keep the declared contents separate from counted and accepted quantities even if the first pilot uses a spreadsheet. [UN/EDIFACT DESADV](https://service.unece.org/trade/untdid/d24a/trmd/desadv_c.htm) · [GS1 EANCOM DESADV](https://www.gs1.org/sites/default/files/docs/eancom/s3/desadv.pdf) · [GS1 EANCOM RECADV](https://www.gs1.org/sites/default/files/docs/eancom/s3/recadv.pdf)

**Exchange observed events when partners need a shared history.** EPCIS defines the event structure. The Core Business Vocabulary supplies terms for steps such as shipping and receiving. An EPCIS aggregation event can describe adding cases to a pallet or removing them. Adopt an EPCIS interface when a customer, traceability program or regulator specifies the required events and granularity. Agree which locations and objects to identify, then test sample events against the published schemas and the recipient’s profile. [GS1 EPCIS/CBV implementation guide](https://ref.gs1.org/guidelines/epcis-cbv/) · [GS1 EPCIS aggregation event](https://ref.gs1.org/epcis/AggregationEvent) · [Schemas and REST interface](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/epcis/2.0.1/artefacts)

## Receiving example and pilot questions

A supplier announces one pallet with 40 cases of cooking oil. The buyer’s item master defines 12 bottles per case. At the dock, the operator scans the pallet SSCC, counts 39 cases of lot L7, and places one leaking case on hold. The receipt records 40 expected, 39 physically received, 38 available and one on hold, all in cases. Available stock rises by 456 bottles. The missing case and the held case have different follow-up actions. Rescanning the pallet to resume the receipt should show the same observations. If ten accepted cases later ship to a store, a lot query should identify that store, 28 available cases and the case on hold.

Before choosing scanner, label or message support, collect sample labels from each packaging level, the supplier’s case conversion, a real mixed-pallet manifest and the buyer’s receiving instructions. Ask who allocates SSCCs, which units arrive without readable labels, how lot/expiry exceptions are resolved, and whether anyone consumes DESADV, RECADV or EPCIS. Test a reprint, a scan with a variable-length lot followed by another AI, and a retransmitted notice against the same receipt.
