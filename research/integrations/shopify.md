# Shopify as the first retail connector

**Pilot decision.** Choose Shopify if the merchant actually takes orders through it, controls the warehouse and store locations in Shopify, and can supply test transactions for checkout and counter sales. Start with merchant-managed locations and a read-only comparison against opening counts and open orders. Enable writes after staff can explain a sale, pickup, refund and transfer across both systems. This is a documentation review conducted on 26 November 2026. A merchant installation and end-to-end tests are still outstanding.

**Version.** Pin Admin GraphQL requests to `/admin/api/2026-10/graphql.json` and the webhook configuration to `2026-10`. Shopify lists this stable version as accessible until **16 October 2027 at 15:00 UTC**. Review release notes before the 2027 pilot and each quarter. Rerun the tests below on the replacement version before the deadline. Log the version returned by Shopify so an automatic fallback is visible. ## Ownership from checkout through handover

| Merchant action | What Shopify records | Proposed WMS action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online checkout | An order records the sale. Routing produces one or more fulfillment orders with assigned locations, delivery methods and line quantities. | Key the order and lines by shop and Shopify IDs. Wait for routing before releasing a pick. Align Shopify’s location priority with the merchant’s rule. Send split, unrecognised, moved or unavailable assignments to staff before picking. [Fulfillment orders](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/objects/FulfillmentOrder) · [Order routing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations/locations/fulfillment) |
| Counter sale | A Shopify POS carry-out uses stock at the selling store. A POS ship-to-customer order follows routing. A register can automatically mark sales fulfilled, and offline orders reach the admin after reconnecting. | Capture the POS sale once. For verified carry-out, post one physical handover at the store. Agree what register action constitutes staff evidence and record the receipt reference. Route ship-later and pickup orders by fulfillment method. Defer mixed carry-out/shipping orders to staff. Show the time of the last sync. [POS location rules](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations/locations/fulfillment) · [POS fulfillment settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/unfulfilled-orders) · [Offline POS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/selling-offline/offline-features) |
| Pickup or carrier handover | `fulfillmentOrderLineItemsPreparedForPickup` marks pickup work ready and automatically notifies the customer. The merchant can mark it picked up in POS or Admin after collection. `fulfillmentCreate` accepts specific fulfillment-order line IDs and quantities for shipping. | Record ready, customer collection, carrier booking and physical dispatch separately. Use the merchant pickup action and reconcile the completed fulfillment to WMS handover evidence. For shipping, publish only the quantities handed to the carrier and retain the tracking reference. After an ambiguous response, inspect existing fulfillments before retrying. [Ready-for-pickup mutation](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/mutations/fulfillmentOrderLineItemsPreparedForPickup) · [Pickup in POS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/pickup-in-store-for-online-orders) · [Create a fulfillment](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/mutations/fulfillmentCreate) |

## Stock at each location

Map the Shopify variant and its inventory item to the WMS SKU and unit. Pair the inventory item with a Shopify location ID to identify the inventory level. Export unmapped, duplicate and untracked items for review. Count stock by location at cutover and import outstanding commitments alongside it. Check that items are active at each selling location. [Inventory objects and states](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/inventory-management-apps/manage-quantities-states)

**A three-unit order against eight units leaves five available.** Shopify commits the three units when the order is placed. The WMS mirrors that demand once against the same location and keeps eight physically on hand until dispatch. Another `available -3` API call would expose only two units for sale. Shopify manages `committed` through order and fulfillment operations. Its separately named `reserved` state can hold stock for other purposes. Match states by their business meaning. The same principle applies to a POS carry-out already reflected in Shopify stock. [Shopify inventory states and order lifecycle](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/inventory-management-apps)

For WMS-originated receipts, damage, counts and transfers, decide which side posts each movement before turning on writes. For a transfer represented only in the WMS, protect approved source units from sale, keep dispatched units unavailable to both sites, and release only the quantity actually received at the destination. If the merchant already posts the same transfer in Shopify, reconcile its ID and quantities first. For authoritative corrections, `inventorySetQuantities` accepts `available` or `on_hand`, requires an idempotency key, and supports comparing the expected prior quantity through `changeFromQuantity`. On a mismatch, reload both ledgers and resolve the cause. A stale snapshot can omit a recent sale even if a retry succeeds. [Inventory quantity management](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/inventory-management-apps/manage-quantities-states) · [Set quantities](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/mutations/inventorySetQuantities)

## Edits, outages and reconciliation

- Subscribe to order creation, edits, updates and cancellations, refunds, routing completion, fulfillment moves, fulfillment changes and inventory-level updates as permitted by the installation. Verify the raw HTTPS body using Shopify’s HMAC. Persist the delivery before acknowledging. Deduplicate by shop and `X-Shopify-Webhook-Id`. The event ID correlates deliveries from one merchant action. Delivery order can vary, so reload the current resource before deciding which stock or fulfillment change is still due. [Webhook topics](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/webhooks/2026-10) · [Delivery verification](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks/verify-deliveries) · [Ordering and recovery](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks)
- On edits and cancellations, compare current lines, assignment, remaining fulfillable quantities and prior WMS handovers. Release only open commitments. Route a cancellation received after picking to staff for physical reversal. A financial refund can arrive without a returned item. Shopify refund lines expose the restock decision and location. Agree that staff hold returned goods until inspection, and reconcile any stock Shopify has already restored before publishing availability. [Order edits](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/editing-orders/considerations) · [Refund line items](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/objects/RefundLineItem)
- Recover missed events with a scheduled, paginated order query filtered by `updated_at`, an overlapping time window and a durable checkpoint advanced after successful processing. Recheck outstanding fulfillment orders and compare counts by inventory item and location. Investigate unexpected deltas, stale POS data, inactive subscriptions and unresolved writes. Show last successful reconciliation, age of differences and affected lines to staff. Older orders outside the default 60-day window need approved `read_all_orders` access or a merchant-supplied opening list. [Orders query](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/queries/orders) · [Order access window](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/objects/Order) · [Webhook troubleshooting](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks/troubleshoot)

## Merchant access and proof

Start with merchant-authorized installation and an offline Admin API token for background reconciliation. Check grants for orders, products, inventory, locations and merchant-managed fulfillment orders (`read_orders`, `read_products`, `read_inventory`, `read_locations`, `read_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders`). Enable `write_inventory` and `write_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders` only for the agreed writes. Confirm required staff permissions, including `fulfill_and_ship_orders`, on the installed shop. Third-party-managed fulfillment orders have different access rules. Confirm distribution type and protected-data access before depending on recipient addresses or contact details. [Access scopes](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/access-scopes) · [Offline access tokens](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/authentication-authorization/access-tokens/offline-access-tokens) · [Protected customer data](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/protected-customer-data)

1. In a test shop, configure the pilot’s two locations, tracked variant, routing, pickup and POS fulfillment settings. Use a checkout test order and a separate authorized counter transaction on a configured POS device. Collect redacted order, line, location and fulfillment-order IDs. Observe actual quantities before and after each action.
2. Walk through eight warehouse units and two store units. Import an online order for three twice. Carry one out at the store. Confirm five available at the warehouse and one at the store. Approve a transfer of three, receive two, and explain the one still in transit. Confirm the destination becomes available only for units received.
3. Repeat with a short pick, edit, cancellation, refund with and without restock, pickup before and after collection, and a disconnected register that later syncs. Drop a webhook and replay another out of order. Interrupt a fulfillment response and confirm a single handover. Capture balances on both sides and any protected-field or permission errors.
4. Proceed to merchant writes after the read-only trial reproduces the commercial orders, location-level promises and handovers. Record differences that require the merchant’s register practice, routing configuration or scope grants to change. A successful synthetic webhook alone cannot establish those facts.
