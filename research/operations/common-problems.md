# When a warehouse record looks wrong

Start with the operator's symptom and reconstruct the last known physical and digital events. A picker at an empty shelf may be seeing a wrong location label, a unit conversion error, an earlier unrecorded pick, or an order already reserved elsewhere. Each cause calls for a different repair. These are failure patterns to test with merchants. Their relative frequency in Venezuela is unmeasured.

| What staff see | What to check first | Recovery and prevention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “The screen says eight, the shelf has none.” | Compare exact variant, bin, condition, unit and reserved quantity. Check pending moves, staged orders and the last actual count. | Report the short pick and locate the goods before posting an adjustment. Keep the next picker's instruction current. [Work exceptions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/warehousing/work-exceptions-log) |
| “We have enough in the warehouse, but this order won't reserve.” | Distinguish site-wide totals from the required bin, lot, owner, condition and existing commitments. | Show why units are ineligible and which alternate stock a worker can request. [Dynamics location reservation example](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/dynamics-365/supply-chain/warehousing/error-cannot-update-quantity-with-new-dimensions) |
| “The right number of shirts arrived, but the sizes are wrong.” | Reconcile by sellable variant and source alias. Check the garment as well as the scanned label. | Keep the two quantity differences and correct the mapping or labels using evidence. [Catalog and units](catalog-and-units.md) |
| “A sale or return moved stock twice.” | Match channel, sale, line, posting and physical handover. Distinguish an honest second scan from a delivery retry or a POS and ERP both posting. | Freeze the affected outbound update if needed. Apply a linked correction and replay the integration against stable identities. [Merchant API](../integrations/merchant-api.md) |
| “I scanned one item and the receipt shows hundreds.” | Check the focused field, preexisting digits, scanner suffix, draft quantity and whether a stock posting occurred. A simulated scan of `421` appended to a quantity of `1` produced 1,421 in a baseline form. | Correct the draft or issue a linked correction for a committed receipt. Rehearse scanning after manual entry and after switching tasks. [Scan-focus trial](../experience/scan-focus-trial.md) |
| “Yesterday's count was correct until the tills synced.” | Match each register's sale and actual handover to the count cutoff. Two registers can reuse a receipt number. | Preserve both sales, post only movements outside the count and keep an ambiguous balance provisional until staff reconcile it. [Opening inventory trial](opening-inventory.md) |
| “Both customers got a pickup confirmation for the last unit.” | Compare the two different command IDs, reservation outcomes, location and any stale or offline register. | Reconcile actual custody and assign the unmet promise. Rehearse competing reservations at commit. [Race conditions](../architecture/race-conditions.md) |
| “Goods left one store and never appeared at the other.” | Match departure and arrival by transfer line, source, destination, unit and actual quantity. | Show in-transit quantity and owner. Record partial receipt and investigate any missing units. [Store transfers](store-replenishment.md) |
| “The carrier has a guide but the box is still here.” | Check the label, staged package and actual collection receipt or manifest. | Separate booking from departure. Reuse the recovered guide and document the actual handover. [Carrier handoffs](../integrations/venezuela-carriers.md) |
| “An adjustment fixed the total, but the error came back.” | Reconstruct the movement history and check whether a summary disagrees with its own postings, whether the physical count differs, or whether a source is still duplicating events. | Repair the failing mechanism. A derived balance can be rebuilt from verified transactions. A physical discrepancy needs observation and attribution. [Dynamics consistency checks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/inventory/on-hand-consistency-check) |

At the pilot, ask for three recent corrections. For each, record time until someone noticed, number of orders or units affected, who could diagnose it, time to resolve and recurrence. Use those figures to prioritize the next intervention. A frequent wrong-size pick might justify better shelf markings sooner than a new automation.
