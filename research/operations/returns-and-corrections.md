# Returns, exchanges and dispatch corrections

At the returns counter, ask which item came back, how many units staff can account for, where they are, and what the customer was promised. Each answer can change independently. The POS or sales channel owns the commercial decision. The WMS records physical custody, condition and availability. The billing system records any credit document. ERPNext illustrates why the integration needs explicit ownership. A return delivery note can receive goods, and a credit note can also update stock if configured. Processing both as independent receipts would double the balance. [ERPNext sales returns](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-return) · [Shopify returns and exchanges](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns)

## What staff should post

| Situation | Commercial action | Stock and custody action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer cancels pickup before collection | Channel confirms cancellation and its refund decision. | Release an unstaged, uncollected commitment once. For a staged pickup, cancellation and handover must claim the same remaining fulfillment quantity in the stock transaction. Keep the bag unavailable while staff verify custody, then post its return to a saleable bin. If collection already occurred, use the return workflow. Reconcile any POS restock. [Shopify cancellation settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/canceling-orders) |
| Price concession or refund while customer keeps goods | Record refund or credit against the original sale. | Record zero physical movement. Keep the refund reference so a later physical return can be matched. [ERPNext price-only credit](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-returns-and-credit-notes-problems) |
| Return authorized or carrier collected a parcel | Record approved lines, expected quantities and return reference. | Track the carrier reference separately. At arrival, count the contents into the receiving location’s hold area. A label, tracking scan or empty parcel provides no item-level receiving quantity. [Shopify processing returns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns/processing-returns) |
| Garment exchanged at a different store | POS links the exchange to the original sale and collects or credits the price difference. | Receive the actual size and colour into hold at the store accepting it. After inspection, release it there or record damage. Reserve and issue the replacement variant at its actual source. A blue M coming in and a blue L going out changes two stock balances, even at the same price. [Shopify POS exchanges](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/exchange) |
| Sealed bag of edible ice returned by a customer | The merchant can decide on refund or replacement independently. | Record receipt, condition and available lot information in hold. A seal alone gives no evidence of temperature or handling during customer custody. Use the merchant’s approved food-safety procedure and an authorized disposition. If discarded, record quantity, reason and actor. Offer a countable-goods pilot only where the operator can satisfy applicable food controls. [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pl/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf) |

At receipt, link the source order and line, original handover, received variant and unit, quantity, receiving site, actor and time. Track the decision on each received quantity through release, continued hold, supplier return or disposal. Queue unknown receipts, wrong variants, conflicting source restocks and quantities above the remaining returnable amount for review. Staff can record the physical arrival while its ownership is unresolved. The connector needs distinct references for receipt, disposition, refund and replacement so replaying one cannot repeat the others. [ERPNext linked return quantities](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-return) · [Shopify partial returns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns/processing-returns)

## A shortage reported after the truck has left

Suppose the warehouse posted a dispatch of six shirts and the customer reports receiving five. Preserve the original dispatch and record a one-unit discrepancy against the line and shipment. Ask the warehouse to check the staging area and pick record. Ask the carrier for its collection or delivery evidence, including what its signature actually covers. A signature for one sealed parcel establishes receipt of a parcel. It leaves the number of shirts inside unresolved.

- **One shirt found in staging.** After checking its identity, post a linked correction reducing the original dispatch by one. The WMS now shows five dispatched and one physically at source. Keep that shirt committed if the merchant agrees to ship it later. Record any later handover under a new dispatch reference.
- **Six units verified at departure.** Keep six dispatched. Let the carrier and merchant investigate the missing unit and record any claim or approved loss against the shipment. If the merchant promises a replacement, create a linked demand and record its eventual dispatch. Increase warehouse stock only upon evidence of an actual return.
- **Evidence still incomplete.** Leave the discrepancy open with an owner, next check and customer contact reference. The merchant can issue a refund while the physical investigation proceeds. Expose the unresolved quantity in the shift handover. A channel that restricts edits to fulfilled quantities may require its own correction workflow. [Shopify order edit limits](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/editing-orders/considerations)

## Operator acceptance

At Store B, exchange a blue M sold by Store A for a blue L. Give Store B two available Ls and no Ms at the start. Before inspection it should have one available L and one held M. After an authorized release it should have one available unit of each. Replay the POS exchange and check both balances. Have two clerks race cancellation and collection with different command IDs, then reverse their order. Only one may settle the same quantity. If a device may have recorded an offline handover, keep stock unavailable until the bag and pending observations are reconciled. Also return a sealed ice bag directly to hold and test both outcomes of the six-shirt discrepancy. Ask a different employee to identify the site, physical owner of the next step, commercial decision, and evidence needed to close each line.

The pilot can demonstrate these workflows with countable goods. Product-specific inspection, recall, temperature history or controlled disposal may require additional capability and local verification before onboarding the merchant.
