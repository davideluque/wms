# Product principles

- **Keep the record true.** Let people record what actually happened, even when it differs from the plan or needs a decision later.
- **People can recover from mistakes.** Make it easy to undo or correct a wrong action. A second tap or retry should be safe.
- **Work survives interruptions.** Save progress as people go. Anyone taking over should be able to see where work stopped and what comes next.
- **Make everyday work easy.** Give frequent tasks a short, predictable path. Ask for extra detail only when the situation calls for it.
- **Say what the software knows.** Make it clear what is confirmed, what is uncertain, and how current the information is.
- **Support the whole job.** When a step needs human judgment or manual work, give people a way to track it through to resolution.
- **Make each entry count.** Carry information forward so the next person who needs it can act without asking for it again.
